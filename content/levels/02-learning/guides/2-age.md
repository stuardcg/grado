# Hint: age is trickier than it looks

`2027 - birth_year` is wrong for anyone whose birthday comes *after* race day.

Someone born on 15 March 2009 is still **17** on 14 March 2027, not 18. Try your function with:

- born 2009-03-14 → should be 18 (birthday is race day)
- born 2009-03-15 → should be 17
