Three small ideas make the difference between an app that grows and one that breaks:

1. **A list of records.** 9 riders or 900, same code.
2. **Rules as data.** Categories live in a table, not in a pile of if/else.
3. **Functions.** Something goes in, something comes out.

You'll learn them by building a tiny script that counts riders per category.

### The habit: let the AI explain, then explain it back

When the AI explains something, don't just nod. Say it back in your own words. If you can't, you've
found exactly what to ask next.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Explain dictionaries." | "Explain dictionaries in 3 lines with a rider example, then ask me to explain it back." |
| "I don't get it." | "I get the loop part. I don't get why we need `age_on`. Why not use the birth year?" |
