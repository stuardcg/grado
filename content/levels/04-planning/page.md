Now you'll turn your notes into a **plan**: what data the app keeps, what the organizer sees, and the
small steps to build it.

The AI writes the first draft. **You're the reviewer.** Your job is to question it, cut it, and change
it until you'd bet on it.

### The habit: make the AI plan before it codes

Ask for the plan first, and say so explicitly. Code written from a plan you reviewed is code you can
follow later.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Build the registration app." | "Don't write code yet. Propose a plan.md with a data model, screens, and 5–8 small steps. Wait for my review." |
| "Looks good." | "Step 4 looks too big. Split it so each part ends in something I can run." |
