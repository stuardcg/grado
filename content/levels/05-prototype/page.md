Today the real app comes to life. It will be **ugly, and that's fine**. What matters is how you build it.

Follow your plan **one step at a time**. After every step, run the app and see it work. When something
breaks, you'll know exactly which change broke it.

### The habit: one small step at a time, run after each

Big requests produce big messes that are hard to debug. Small requests produce small, checkable wins.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Build everything in the plan." | "Do only step 2 of plan.md, then stop so I can run it." |
| "It doesn't work." | "I ran `python -m app.summary registrations.csv` and got this error: (full error)" |

### You're done when

- the app loads the sample registrations and prints the right summary
- the window shows the riders and can print a start list PDF
