# Hint: the tricky rows

The sample export has traps on purpose. If your counts are off, look at these rows:

- Two rows with the **same email**, written differently (capital letters, spaces).
- A row with the same email as an earlier one, but **missing something required**. Is it a duplicate,
  or rejected?
- A birth date that **doesn't exist** on the calendar.
- Riders whose birthday is **race day itself**.
