Before anyone builds anything, you're going to **interview the organizer**.

Your coach will play {{project.organizer}}, who runs {{project.event_name}}. Ask her anything about the
event, the riders, and what she needs. Write what you learn in `notes/investigation.md`.

### The habit: ask questions before asking for code

The invoice app showed what happens when nobody asks first: a design that can't grow. Here, the better
your questions, the better {{project.organizer}}'s answers. Vague questions get vague answers, on purpose.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about the app." | "Who will use the app, and on which days?" |
| "What data do you need?" | "What does one registration contain? Which parts are required?" |
| "Any problems?" | "What went wrong with registrations last year?" |

### You're done when your notes cover

- who uses the app, and when
- what one registration contains
- what can go wrong
