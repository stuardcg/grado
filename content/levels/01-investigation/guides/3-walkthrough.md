# Walkthrough: finding what can go wrong

A good trick: take **one field at a time** and ask "how could this be wrong?"

- **Email** → missing, typo, no @, same email twice.
- **Birth date** → missing, impossible date, wrong format, rider too young.
- **Age** → on which day is age counted? What about a rider whose birthday is race day?
- **Emergency contact** → empty.
- **The whole row** → the same rider twice.

Then ask {{project.organizer}} which of these really happen and what she wants done about each one.
