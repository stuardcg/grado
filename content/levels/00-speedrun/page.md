Every journey starts with the shortcut everyone takes: ask the AI for the whole thing at once.

Today you'll do exactly that, **on purpose**. Write one prompt asking for the registration app,
let the AI build it, try it for a few minutes, and keep it **exactly as it comes out**. No fixing.

Why? At the finish line, the organizer will ask for a change. You'll make it in this app *and* in the
one you build step by step. You'll feel the difference yourself.

### The habit: notice what one big prompt gives you

Pay attention to what you get: what works, what's confusing, and what you wouldn't know how to change.
That's all you need to bring back.

### How long

About 30 minutes. That's the whole point of a speedrun.
